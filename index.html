FAQ

Q: What is this photobooth?

A: This is a fan-made photobooth by MYPlliDduks, created for PLLIs to enjoy the frames we have designed for our fan events and projects.

Q: Are these official PLAVE frames?

A: No. All frames featured on this website are fan-made designs created by MYPlliDduks for our own events and projects. This website is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to PLAVE or their management.

Q: Will new frames be added regularly?

A: Not necessarily. The frames on this website are mainly designs we have created for our past events, along with frames we may create for future projects. This website is not intended to be a continuously updated template library.

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and want you to feel comfortable using our photobooth.

Photos: We do not collect, upload, or store your photos. Your photos are processed locally in your browser and remain on your device.

Camera Access: Camera permission is used only during your photo session. We do not access your camera outside of the photobooth experience.

Personal Information: We do not require you to provide any personal information to use our photobooth.

Facial Recognition: We do not use facial recognition or face analysis, and we do not collect or store biometric data.
